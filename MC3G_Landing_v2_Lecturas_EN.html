05

Campaign No. 05 · Field diagnostics

Campaign records, not sales cases

Four real, anonymized examples of work: no dam, company, or client named.

RECORD 01Inclinometer

Reconciling probe series vs. vendor series

A forensic audit on a CFRD dam that compared the raw probe-motor series against the vendor's processed output. It identified an axis sign inversion and a possible half-step shift in depth attribution — without forcing an artificial rescale just to make the series "match."

RECORD 02Piezometers

Calibration bias disguised as acceleration

A ~5 mm jump coinciding with a probe change read, at first glance, as pore-pressure acceleration. Residual analysis identified it as calibration bias between probes, and an overlap campaign was recommended to confirm it before escalating it as a dam-safety alert.

RECORD 03Settlement cells

An "outlier" that was actually a startup period

57 readings with settlements beyond −25 cm had been flagged as outliers to discard. Reconstructing the timeline revealed a real, months-long startup instability period — discarding it would have contaminated the settlement-vs-fill correlation for the whole downstream analysis.

RECORD 04Anomaly detection

Two methods, two results — the specialist decides, not the algorithm

On the same piezometer series, a simple statistical band flagged 5 readings as anomalous and an Isolation Forest flagged 7, with only 3 readings in common between the two. No algorithm was used as an automatic verdict: the result was delivered as input for the specialist to classify case by case.
