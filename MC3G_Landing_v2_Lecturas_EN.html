Reading N.º 05 · Field Findings

Field logs, not sales pitches

Four real, anonymized work examples: no dam, company, or client ever named.

LOG 01Inclinometer

Reconciling probe vs. vendor series

Forensic audit on a CFRD dam comparing the raw probe-motor series against the vendor's processed output. Identified an axis sign inversion and a possible half-step shift in depth attribution — without forcing an artificial rescale to make the series "match."

LOG 02Piezometers

Calibration bias disguised as acceleration

A ~5 mm jump coinciding with a probe change initially read as pore-pressure acceleration. Residual analysis identified it as a calibration bias between probes, and an overlap campaign was recommended to confirm it before escalating it as a dam-safety alert.

LOG 03Settlement Cells

An "outlier" that was actually a startup period

57 readings with settlements beyond −25 cm had been flagged as outliers to discard. Reconstructing the timeline showed a genuine, months-long startup instability period — discarding it would have contaminated the settlement-fill correlation for the entire downstream analysis.

LOG 04Anomaly Detection

Two methods, two results — the specialist decides, not the algorithm

On the same piezometer series, a simple statistical band flagged 5 readings as anomalous and an Isolation Forest flagged 7, with only 3 overlapping. No algorithm was used as an automatic verdict: the output was handed over as input for the specialist to classify case by case.
