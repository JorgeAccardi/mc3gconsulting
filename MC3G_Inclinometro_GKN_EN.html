GKN Inclinometer Analyzer — profiles, shear zone, and rates

Upload one or several .gkn files (one file = one campaign). We compute cumulative and incremental deflection per A and B axis, locate the candidate shear zone by local kink (not by absolute maximum — that only measures wellhead drag), and the 3 movement rates there.

All analysis happens in your browser. Files are never uploaded to any server.

Drag your .gkn files here (you can drop several at once), or click to choose them

Wellhead elevation (m a.s.l.) — optional

Installation azimuth, A+ axis (° from North) — optional

If provided, we convert A/B (instrument axes) to North/East (real geographic axes) and compute the movement vector with a real compass direction.

This tool identifies candidates and computes metrics — it does not replace a specialist's judgment. Candidate shear zones, movement rates, and any alert shown here must be confirmed by a qualified dam-safety engineer before any operational decision. AI assists, the specialist classifies.

KPIs

All-campaigns view (Sitemaster style)

Same format as the vendor's report: each campaign in its own color, with a full legend by date and file. Hover to see each campaign's value at that depth; mouse wheel to zoom ("reset zoom" button top-right when zoomed out). Rotated A-A'/B-B' uses our standard azimuth rotation (North/East Component) — it does not reproduce Sitemaster's exact proprietary formula.

The 3 rates — at the critical depth

Instantaneous rate

Between each pair of consecutive readings, mm/day. Red = advancing, green = retreating.

Monthly rate

Between the last reading of each month and the last reading of the previous month, mm/day.

Overall rate

Between the first reading and each subsequent reading — how the cumulative average evolves, mm/day.

Summary table (vendor style)

One row per campaign at the critical depth: date, Cum.A/Cum.B, resultant vector, direction, and the 3 rates accumulated to that date. N° is our own sequential index, not the vendor's internal numbering.
