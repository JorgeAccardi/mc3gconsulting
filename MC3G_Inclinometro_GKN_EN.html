GKN Inclinometer Analyzer — profiles, shear zone, and rates

Upload one or several .gkn files (one file = one campaign). We compute cumulative and incremental deflection per A and B axis, locate the candidate shear zone by local kink (not by absolute maximum — that only measures wellhead drag), and the 3 movement rates there.

All analysis happens in your browser. Files are never uploaded to any server.

This tool identifies candidates and computes metrics — it does not replace a specialist's judgment. Candidate shear zones, movement rates, and any alert shown here must be confirmed by a qualified dam-safety engineer before any operational decision. AI assists, the specialist classifies.

KPIs

Cum.A, Cum.B + Chk.A, Chk.B (checksum)

Cum.A/Cum.B are 2 of Sitemaster's 4 standard profiles. Rot.A/Rot.B (the other 2, Sitemaster's proprietary values) are not reproduced here — instead, further down, North/East Component using the standard geotechnical rotation. Depth on the vertical axis (0 at top) · gray = all campaigns · dotted = reference · red = current campaign · red horizontal line = candidate shear zone (Cum.A/Cum.B only).

All-campaigns view (Sitemaster style)

Same format as the vendor's report: each campaign in its own color, with a full legend by date and file. Rotated A-A'/B-B' uses our standard azimuth rotation (North/East Component) — it does not reproduce Sitemaster's exact proprietary formula.

Incremental profile — A axis and B axis

Not part of Sitemaster's standard report — we add it because a sharp, localized spike here is the clearest signature of a real localized kink, when the cumulative profile alone may not show it as clearly.
